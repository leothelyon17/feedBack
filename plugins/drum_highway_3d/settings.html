3D Drum Highway

Pick a song with a drum tab (sloppak-spec §5.3), choose 3D Drum Highway from the viz picker. Profile name, MIDI device, and lane order live under Profiles on this tab. MIDI note mapping lives on the MIDI tab. Bloom, camera, and theme live on the Graphics tab.

Chart fallbacks

When a song uses a piece that is not on this profile's lanes, route it to one of those lanes, or set "—" to silently drop it. Lane order itself is edited under Profiles.

Chart fallbacks for pieces I don't have

When a song uses a piece that isn't on your kit, route it to one of your lanes. Set "—" to silently drop that piece.
